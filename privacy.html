VowenLite privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026.

VowenLite is a personal app run by its owner on their own computer. If the owner connects it to Google, it asks for access to Google Tasks and to Google Calendar events.

What it accesses and why

It reads and writes the owner's tasks and calendar events only to keep them in sync between Google and the owner's PC. Nothing else in the Google account is accessed.

Where data goes

Tasks and events are stored in a file on the owner's computer. The Google sign-in token is stored on the same computer. Data is not sent to any server operated by the developer, and it is not sold, shared, or used for advertising or to train AI models.

Control and removal

The owner can disconnect Google inside the app, delete the local files, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions at any time.
